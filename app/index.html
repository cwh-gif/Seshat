About

Draw a hieroglyph and the app matches it against the 769 canonical Gardiner signs using a neural encoder that runs locally in your browser — no drawing ever leaves your device.

After the first visit it works offline. Going online again picks up app updates and may ask you to sign back in.

Recognizer trained on the Hand-drawn Hieroglyph Dataset (Aneesh et al., IC4 2024); canonical glyphs and sign data from the ArchaeoHack starter pack. Match quality is best for complete, single-sign drawings.
